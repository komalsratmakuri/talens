SHAP Mathematical Formulation

Each candidate evaluation computes an additive feature attribution explanation around the baseline prior probability E[f(x)] = 0.50:

P(Shortlist | x) = clamp( E[f(x)] + Σ φᵢ(x), 0.04, 0.98 )
  • Positive φᵢ (> 0): Skills, quantified telemetry, seniority match, and pedigree pushing the candidate toward recruiter shortlist.
  • Negative φᵢ (< 0): Missing mandatory infrastructure keywords, 60–90 day notice period penalties, or unquantified bullet points.
  • Counterfactual Recourse: Identifies minimal-effort edits (Δx) needed to cross the 0.65 pass boundary.
